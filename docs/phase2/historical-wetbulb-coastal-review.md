# Research memo: coastal hourly wet-bulb source selection (Top 50)

**Status:** 2026-09-30, research only. No source substitution, composite high, page asset, merge or release is approved here. The samples below are **not** 1950–present extrema. Keep source ZIPs, hourly values, checksums and detailed reports outside Git in `~/.local/share/wetbulb35/historical-wbt/private/`.

## Cohort screen and real comparison

- A sequential, restartable 48-UTC-hour ERA5-Land source-cell screen (1950-02-28 through 1950-03-01) covered **all 50 frozen ranked routes**: **44 fully valid, 6 entirely masked, 0 partial/untested**. The masked nearest cells are Chennai (#1), Singapore (#10), Dar es Salaam (#28), Luanda (#30), Rio de Janeiro (#39), and Mogadishu (#50). This does not prove full-history completeness or predict the masked proportion of the 130,686-city inventory.
- All six masked cities have an *available 1950 sample* at a candidate ERA5-Land land cell within 15 km that passed the pinned Natural Earth 1:10m same-country/same-landmass/straight-land-path screen: Chennai 8.61 km, Singapore 5.68 km, Dar es Salaam 8.11 km (adjacent tile), Luanda 8.30 km, Rio 12.08 km, Mogadishu 8.52 km. These remain **manual-review candidates** (`publishedCell: null`); a full-period and terrain check has not happened. A closer 8.45-km Rio candidate was rejected because the straight path crosses the bay.
- Bounded official ERA5 **single-level hourly time-series** point requests returned all **48 simultaneous hourly temperature, dew point and surface-pressure values** for those six cities plus Delhi and Houston controls over the same dates. Actual ERA5 grid cells and ERA5-Land candidate cells were paired by identical UTC hour and evaluated with the *same pinned Romps calculation*. The mean signed difference (ERA5 minus ERA5-Land, °C) over those **48 hours only** was Chennai +0.06, Singapore +0.16, Dar es Salaam −0.20, Luanda **+1.59**, Rio +0.03, Mogadishu +0.32; the Delhi and Houston controls were +0.94 and +0.56. The values cannot be extrapolated to annual or historical highs. Luanda in particular shows why an arbitrary average or maximum across models is not a defensible city record.
- A second bounded 48-hour comparison in **2025-07-18–19** covered all six masked cities, with valid simultaneous T/dewpoint/pressure in each ERA5 grid point and its nearby ERA5-Land candidate. Mean paired ERA5-minus-ERA5-Land wet-bulb differences (°C) were Chennai **+0.80**, Singapore **+0.30**, Dar es Salaam **+0.37**, Luanda **+0.33**, Rio **+1.16**, Mogadishu **+0.79**. Luanda shifted from +1.59 in the 1950 sample to +0.33, while Rio shifted from +0.03 to +1.16. These opposite-season, different-era snapshots demonstrate that a universal fixed correction is unsupported; they still do **not** establish long-term biases, station accuracy or historical extrema.
- The actual ERA5 `land_sea_mask` static field (0 = all sea, 1 = all land) at all six grid points confirms mixed cells: **Chennai 0.6009, Singapore 0.3328, Dar es Salaam 0.4958, Luanda 0.3889, Rio 0.3537, Mogadishu 0.5850**. A separate rough Natural Earth 1:10m land-outline screen places the ERA5 *grid-point centers* for Singapore, Luanda and Rio on water (other three masked-city ERA5 centers on land); all six 0.25° footprints include both land and sea in this approximation. The outline is independent of and less authoritative than the model mask. ERA5 grids are coarser (~31 km; regular output 0.25°) than ERA5-Land (~9 km). A value at a water-centered or mixed-footprint grid point is not automatically an on-land city measurement.
- A separate 10m coastline check found 24 city coordinates within 15 km of a land boundary and two more (Lagos, Miami) slightly outside the mapped land polygon, despite valid ERA5-Land grid cells. This is a **review queue**, not 26 confirmed errors: coordinate precision, inlets, lakes and the external coastline geometry can disagree. Valid model cells near coasts also warrant spatial review.
- Nearby NOAA ISD station records do not solve a full-history missing-cell problem by themselves. In 1950, sampled station CSVs contained only 673 records for Paya Lebar, 318 for Mogadishu, 67 for Dar es Salaam and 301 for Luanda; selected nearby Chennai/Rio station IDs returned no 1950 CSV. Inventory date spans and partial station hours are not a validated 1950–present daily-maximum series. Station pressure/altimeter fields also vary by year; observed-station results need their own coverage and pressure-method gates.

## Method decision to test, not a blended record

1. **Default:** nearest ERA5-Land cell if it has simultaneous complete T/dew-point/surface-pressure history, with a coastal geography review if it lies close to sea, an inlet or a questionable city coordinate.
2. **Masked nearest cell:** search adjacent 0.1° tiles for the nearest complete same-landmass cell within the approved 15 km limit; check country, straight over-land path, terrain, original-point identity and the *full* covered period. If validated, use **that ERA5-Land cell as the primary source** and disclose distance/method. Do not silently treat its modeled maximum as an official observation.
3. **ERA5 comparison:** calculate wet bulb independently from simultaneous ERA5 variables at its *actual* 0.25° grid point. Compare paired hourly and local-daily values with the chosen ERA5-Land cell in early/late years and opposing seasons; inspect the **official ERA5 land-sea-mask fraction** before asserting whether a mixed cell represents land. Use station observations only where quality-passed simultaneous inputs exist, as an external check—not as fillers. ERA5-Land used a preliminary ERA5 back extension before 1979, so the two model series are not guaranteed to differ by a stationary offset.
4. **Disagreement or no suitable land cell:** do **not** average the two model maxima, choose their larger maximum, or splice hours into one 1950–present number. A calibrated same-hour blend or downscaling model could be considered only with independent station validation across seasons, eras and held-out extreme days; the sparse station checks above cannot establish one. If validated evidence supports an ERA5-only coastal proxy, publish it as a **separate, explicitly labeled modeled method/period** with a site-specific uncertainty explanation; otherwise show the high as unavailable. A generic “unclear” badge alone must not hide a changed data source or imply a station record. Set actual discrepancy and publication thresholds after cross-season/year paired comparisons, not from this two-day sample.
5. **Scale-up:** classify source-cell identity and static mask once per distinct cell; deduplicate by cell and IANA timezone. Screen coastline/terrain proximity separately, then review exceptions in bounded regional batches. The 6/50 result is not a global frequency estimate. Benchmark acquisition, full-period coverage, reproducibility, derivative size, Worker memory and staging before the Top 200 and remaining canonical routes.
